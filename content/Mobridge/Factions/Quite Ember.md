#Faction 
Motto: “We don’t burn. We smolder.”

Operating Base: The Old Quarry Tunnels beneath the western ridge of Ash Hollow.

Size: 12–15 active members, with scattered sympathizers across the region.

Primary Goal: Undermine Coalition intelligence operations and prevent Ash Hollow from falling under Coalition occupation.

Secondary Goal: Protect the town’s ley nexus and magical residents from exploitation or extermination.

💀 ORIGIN

The Quiet Ember was formed seven years ago when a Coalition recon unit attempted to “survey” Ash Hollow’s ley lines. Several townsfolk were executed for “arcane contamination,” including two council members.

In the aftermath, a small group of survivors began coordinating counter-operations—sabotage, misinformation, and resource smuggling. Their leader, Rian Doss, is a former CS Scout who deserted after witnessing a massacre of civilians during a ley storm incident.

🩸 MEMBERS OF THE QUIET EMBER

⚙️ METHODS OF RESISTANCE

- Intercepting Coalition Comm Relays: They jam or misdirect transmissions from scouting patrols, feeding false terrain data.
- Supply Network Manipulation: Through Talla’s forged IDs, the group diverts CS shipments to local farmers and merchants.
- Targeted Sabotage: Never overt — instead of blowing bridges, they make weapons fail or armor jam at critical moments.
- Psychic Counterintelligence: Mira Thorn cloaks psychic signatures, giving the illusion of an empty valley.
- Rumor Warfare: The Quiet Ember deliberately spreads conflicting stories about Ash Hollow — making it appear cursed, unstable, or irrelevant.

🔥 WHY THEY DON’T TRUST OTHER RESISTANCE GROUPS

1. Past Betrayal: Two years ago, they tried working with the Free Chi-Town Rebellion Cell. The result was a raid that got three members executed and nearly exposed the ley reactor. They never learned who leaked their position.
2. Philosophical Divide: The Quiet Ember believes in quiet, surgical resistance — no open war, no grand statements. Other groups glorify martyrdom and revolution. To them, that’s suicide.
3. Moral Ambiguity: They’ve accepted jobs from smugglers, mercenaries, even Horizon Co. itself — anything that keeps the CS out. That’s made them “tainted” in the eyes of other purist rebels.
4. Local Loyalty: Their oath is to Ash Hollow, not the wider anti-CS movement. They believe most resistance factions are pawns of larger powers, not true protectors of the frontier.

💣 RELATIONSHIP TO ASH HOLLOW

- Mayor Thalia Vorn knows something is happening under her town but looks away — plausible deniability keeps her in office.
- Zek the Shifter disapproves of them but occasionally passes them ley warnings when CS scouts get too close.
- Dr. Quell secretly provides tech upgrades, pretending it’s “for field testing.”
- Most townsfolk think the Quiet Ember are ghost stories told by smugglers.

🎲 GM HOOKS

- “False Flag”: A rival resistance group impersonates the Quiet Ember to provoke a CS strike on Ash Hollow.
- “Echo Code”: Rian’s encrypted CS transmissions begin predicting future events — or manipulating him.
- “The Broken Deal”: Patchwork steals a data core from Horizon Co., but it starts broadcasting psychic distress calls.
- “The Burn”: A CS task force closes in. The Quiet Ember must decide: flee, fight, or burn the evidence — including the ley stabilizer that keeps Ash Hollow intact
