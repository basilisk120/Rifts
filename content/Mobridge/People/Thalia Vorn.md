**Appearance**

Thalia Vorn cuts a hard silhouette — square shoulders, scarred hands, and a mane of coarse white hair that she refuses to dye. Her left eye is cybernetic, glowing faint amber under her brow; the right one is cold and sharp as flint.

She dresses in heavy dusters patched with Coalition armor plates, wears no badge (“If they don’t know I’m in charge, they ain’t from here”), and almost always has a half-smoked cigar clenched in her teeth.

The smell of ash and ozone follows her everywhere — a side effect of years breathing ley storms and battlefield fumes.

Looking to protect the town at any cost

[[Echoed]]
Denies their existence, but keeps a cigar box filled with “cold quartz” — a local charm to keep the Echoed at bay. The presence of the child-Echo near her suggests she’s haunted by personal loss.
