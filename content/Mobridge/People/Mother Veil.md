#Echoed #NPC 
Leader of the [[Echoed]] cult

- Alignment: Diabolic (delusional visionary)
- Appearance: Veiled in metallic silk, eyes glowing with reflected ley light.
- Motive: “Awaken” the ley nexus beneath Ash Hollow, believing it’s a sleeping god. Working to let the god in.  Knows it is malevolent
- Lives in a cave in the "old mines" 
- Hooks: Sends acolytes to steal power cores, artifacts, and psychic conduits.
- Secret: Her mind is linked to the Rift itself — she sometimes speaks in other voices.
