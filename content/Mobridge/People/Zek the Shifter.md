#NPC #Echoed 
Dimension-walking mystic living in the Warrens

Wandered into town one and just stayed

Zek is a tall, lean human whose form seems to shimmer slightly at the edges, as if reality has trouble deciding which version of him belongs here. His skin bears faint ley-line scars—bioluminescent traceries that pulse when the Rift flares.

He dresses in layered robes of patchwork cloth and dimensional fabric, fastened with bits of circuitry and bone. His right arm is entirely replaced by a biomechanical construct of alien origin — fluid metal and living rune.

Faction: [[Echoed]]
Believes the Echoed are fragments of his failed bindings. Keeps a warded circle near The Sink to prevent them from manifesting during storms. The Echoed seem drawn to his aura.
Does he believe? Is he trying to help or hinder the awakening.
