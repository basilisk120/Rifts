**Appearance**
Quell is tall, almost gaunt, with translucent, faintly glowing skin through which circuitry-like ley conduits can be seen pulsing faintly.

His eyes are luminous teal, with no visible pupils; they sometimes flicker in sync with the town’s power grid.

His voice carries a strange resonance, as though a second voice — digital or spiritual — echoes half a syllable behind his own.

He dresses in a patched long coat with crystalline trim, the lining stitched with insulation runes.

When he’s concentrating, the air around him vibrates softly, like a transformer about to blow.
