#NPC #DustChapel
Occupation: Keeper of the [[The Dust Chapel]], spiritual counselor, Rift-watcher

Apparent Age: 40s, though she seems ageless in dim light

Race: Human (possibly augmented by spiritual or ley exposure)

Alignment: Principled, but pragmatic

Residence: The small rectory behind the Chapel, lined with relics sealed in dustproof glass

Sister Vellia wears the gray-brown robes of an old desert order — practical, unadorned. Her eyes are a pale gold that reflect ley light strangely, and her skin carries a faint shimmer when the Rift flares. Her voice is soft, almost hypnotic; when she recites prayers, the air vibrates as if echoing her words.

She carries a set of rosary-like prayer beads carved from ley-bleached quartz — each bead etched with a sigil of protection. When she prays over someone, the beads glow faintly blue.

- emperament: Patient, eerily calm, but sharp with insight; she misses nothing.
- Philosophy: “Faith is the dust left behind after the storm — proof that something survived.”
- Virtue: Deep empathy; she takes confessions from humans, D-Bees, even Echoed.
- Vice: Secret fatalism — she believes Ash Hollow will eventually be consumed by the Rift and is quietly preparing for it.
