#NPC #quietEmber

Old Varek - occasionally works at [[Baines Fixery]] and at [[Hollow Salvage Yard]]

Faction: [[Quite Ember]]
Role: Mechanic, saboteur, and demolitions expert

Background: Once ran a repair shop before his son was conscripted and killed by the CS.

Personality: Gruff, fatalistic, protective of the younger members.

Contribution: Builds EMP mines, jury-rigged plasma charges, and vehicle traps disguised as irrigation controls.

Goal: Keep Ash Hollow safe — not just from the CS, but from anyone who would bring war back to the town.
