#location
**Type**: Open bazaar of scavengers, smugglers, and mages

**Manager**: [[Nema Frost]] (Council liaison)

**Description:**

Colorful tarps stretch over cracked ferrocrete, glowing faintly from crystals embedded beneath. Traders sell artifacts still humming with power, food grown in rad-green soil, and anything else that survived a Rift.

- **Specialty Vendors:**

- “Crater Dan’s Alchemical Supplies” – sells tinctures made from mutated flora.
- “Talon’s Trinkets” – a D-Bee jeweler who sets ley stones into charms.
- “The Coil Rack” – black-market weapons & psionic focus items.

- **Rumor:**

- Every full moon, a Rift opens just outside the stalls for a few seconds — some claim merchants from elsewhere appear briefly.
