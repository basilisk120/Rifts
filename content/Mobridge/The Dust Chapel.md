**Type**: Shrine & Psionic Meditation Hall
**Caretaker:** [[Sister Vellia Dane]]
**Description:**
A ruin converted into a temple where the walls hum in resonance with prayers. Followers believe the Rift is divine, and Vellia’s sermons sometimes cause minor telekinetic surges.

**Dedication:** “To those who remained when the world was remade.”

The Dust Chapel is built from scavenged ferrocrete and the bones of a ruined pre-Rifts waystation. Its walls are etched with hundreds of names, carved by hand, covered in desert soot and glowing faintly with ley residue. The chapel is always dim, lit by slow-burning oil lamps filled with Rift-glow.

When the wind moves through the cracks, it sounds like whispers in prayer.

It is not officially recognized by any surviving church — but it is a place of stillness, and somehow, in a town where ley storms rip open the sky, that matters.

**The Dust Chapel’s Role**

**The chapel serves as:**

- Neutral ground — all factions may meet here without bloodshed (by unspoken law).
- Confessional refuge — no one who confesses under the Chapel’s sigil may be harmed for 24 hours.
- Resonant shrine — ley surges pass through it, making its relics hum like tuning forks.
- Hooks:

- PCs attending a service might experience visions of alternate timelines.
- Hidden in the back chamber: a ley crystal heart “gifted” by Leto
- Neutral but potential be able to be bought off by Leto or others
- Quiet ember weapon cache below church believing “the war for freedom is a kind of prayer.”
