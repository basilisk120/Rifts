# Heavy Pulse

Energy weapons fired in this mode are RoF 1, use three Shots, add +1 to the\
Shooting roll, +3 to the damage roll, and apply the Snapfire and Mega Damage qualities.

# Heavy Beam

Energy weapons fired in this mode are RoF 1, use three Shots, add +4 to\
the damage roll, and apply the Snapfire and Mega Damage qualities.

# Bonus Damage

hit with raise adds +1d6! to dmg. (add d6! to dmg modifer)

# Aim

Ignore up to +4 Range, Cover, Called Shot or speed penalties. or add +2 to roll spend turn\
to aim

# Snapfire

When fired from the hip, rather then with scope, bipod or tripod -2 to shooting

# Blast\_Template

SBT - Small Blast Template 2 inches
MBT - Medium Blast Template 4 inches
LBT - Large Blast Template 6 inches

# Reload

Number of actions to reload the next round

# Shotgun

Weapons that spray bullets or buckshot are much more likely to hit others. Each skill die that rolls a 1 or a 2  hits a bystander
+2 to shooting
full damage at short range
-1 die at medium range
-2 die at long range
no extreme range

# Suppressive\_Fire

3 x bullet usage for the [Rate of Fire](rules#ROF)
Always incurs [recoil](rules#recoil) penalty
place a [Medium blast template](rules#Blast_Template) and make a single shooting role reguardless of ROF
compare results to all targets
success means the target is distracted
raise means a hit roll for dmg - no bonus die

# Recoil

-2 penatly to shooting for any [Rate of Fire](rules#ROF)  greater then 1

# Soak\_Roll

After rolling but before applying damage
Spend a benny to "Soak" a roll
Vigor check.\
Each success and raise removed 1 wound.\
if all wounds are removed player may remove the "shaken" effect

# Exposed\_Crew

Crew: 50% chance non-Called\
Shots hit the character instead; on a Crew\
Critical Hit the vehicle’s armor is bypassed.

# Nimble

It is -1 to be hit by any attack it is aware  of and gains +1 on Evasion rolls.

# Scale

When creatures of different Scales attack  each other, the smaller creature adds the  difference between its Scale and its target to  its attacks. A Tiny fairy (-6 Scale modifier),  for example, adds +10 to hurl a bolt at a Huge  dragon (+4).  The larger creature subtracts the difference  from its attacks. A Very Small eagle (-4)  subtracts 2 from its Fighting totals when  attacking a Tiny fairy (-6)
